# Example Health Partners: Information Security Policy

*A fictional policy written for demonstrations. It covers some Security Rule topics well, some partly, and leaves others out on purpose.*

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how Example Health Partners protects electronic protected health information (ePHI). It applies to every employee, contractor and volunteer who creates, receives, maintains or transmits ePHI on our behalf.

## 2. Security responsibility

The Chief Information Security Officer is the designated security official. The security official is responsible for developing and implementing the policies and procedures in this document and for reporting to the executive team each quarter.

## 3. Risk management

We perform a risk analysis of every system that holds ePHI once a year and whenever a major change is made. Risks that are rated high are recorded in the risk register with an owner and a due date, and the register is reviewed by the security official each quarter.

## 4. Access control

### 4.1 Unique user identification
Every user has a unique user ID. Shared or generic accounts are not permitted on systems that hold ePHI.

### 4.2 Authorization
Access to ePHI is granted only after the data owner approves a written request that states the role and the systems needed. Access is limited to the minimum necessary for the role.

### 4.3 Termination
When a person leaves, their accounts are disabled on their last working day and all badges and equipment are collected.

### 4.4 Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must never be shared with anyone.

### 4.5 Automatic logoff
Sessions on workstations that access ePHI are logged off automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity.

## 5. Workstations

Workstations that access ePHI must be located in areas that are not visible to visitors, and screens must be locked when the user steps away. Laptops use full-disk encryption.

## 6. Backups and recovery

Backups of all systems that hold ePHI are taken every night, encrypted, and copied to a secondary site. Restores are tested twice a year.

## 7. Audit logging

Systems that hold ePHI record user logins, record access and changes. Logs are kept for one year.

## 8. Security incidents

Workforce members must report suspected security incidents to the security desk immediately. The security officer investigates every report, records the outcome, and notifies affected parties as required by law.

## 9. Training

All workforce members complete security awareness training when they are hired.

## 10. Vendors

Vendors who handle ePHI must sign a business associate agreement before they receive any data.

## 11. Document control

This policy is reviewed by the security official and approved by the executive team. Policies are kept for six years from the date they were last in effect.
